Sutherland

Getting Here

Invercharron is about an hour north of Inverness, on the east coast of the Highlands of Scotland, south of the Kyle of Sutherland.

by air
Inverness (INV) is the nearest airport, about an hour and a quarter south by road. Edinburgh is roughly four hours and Aberdeen three and a half, both with good onward trains.
hiring a car
The desks are in Arrivals at Inverness Airport - Avis, Budget, Enterprise, Europcar, Hertz and Sixt between them - with anything from a small runabout to something big enough for the whole party.
taxis
Mackays in Dornoch are the ones we would call first. Further afield: Luxury Highland Chauffeur 07561 468143, Inverness Taxis 01463 222 222, and A2B Taxis in Inverness 01463 807060.
by train
The Far North Line runs from Inverness to Ardgay, about a mile and a half from the house - roughly an hour and a quarter. Tain and Lairg are the other useful stops. Trains are few; check the timetable early.
the sleeper
The Caledonian Sleeper leaves London Euston in the evening and arrives in Inverness for breakfast. It is far and away the most agreeable way to come, and it books up a long way ahead.
