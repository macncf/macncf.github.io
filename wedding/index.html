Sutherland

Getting There

Invercharron sits on the south side of the Kyle of Sutherland, a little west of Ardgay and about an hour north of Inverness.

by air
Inverness (INV) is the nearest airport, about an hour and a quarter south by road. Edinburgh is roughly four hours and Aberdeen three and a half, both with good onward trains.
by train
The Far North Line runs from Inverness to Ardgay, about a mile and a half from the house — roughly an hour and a quarter. Tain and Lairg are the other useful stops. Trains are few; check the timetable early.
the sleeper
The Caledonian Sleeper leaves London Euston in the evening and arrives in Inverness for breakfast. It is far and away the most agreeable way to come, and it books up a long way ahead.
by road
The A9 north from Inverness, over the Dornoch Firth bridge, then the A836 at Bonar Bridge. About sixty miles.
